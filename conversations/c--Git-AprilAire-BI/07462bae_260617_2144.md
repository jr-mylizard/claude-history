**You**

How do I delete a space in Notion?

---

**Claude**

To delete a space (workspace) in Notion:

1. Go to **Settings** (click your workspace name in the top-left sidebar)
2. Navigate to **Settings** tab in the left panel
3. Scroll to the bottom of the page to find the **Danger Zone** section
4. Click **Delete entire workspace**
5. Confirm by typing the workspace name when prompted

**Note:** This is irreversible — all pages, databases, and content in that workspace will be permanently deleted. Make sure to export anything you need first via **Settings → Export content**.

If you're looking to delete a **page** rather than a whole workspace, just right-click the page in the sidebar and select **Delete**.
